# The World Today — Monday 23 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026: 31 stories
across 6 regions, led by three hero scenes — the AI rout that hit Asia's
markets (KOSPI -3.3%), Guterres urging global AI safety rules, and the US lead
over China at a record low in the Asia Power Index (gap 5.3).

The front page carries a number strip, a region bar, a "Day in UTC" strip,
10 small story cards, a world map with 31 numbered pins, an "Also on Monday"
list, and a DATE CHECK box that names what is *not* 23 November news, so
nothing in the image contradicts itself. Scenes are drawn illustrations,
not photographs.

`main.py` copies the committed `entry.png` (1024×1024) to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` and verifies it is byte-identical.
